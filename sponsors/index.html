Sponsors

Sponsor the work

I send fixes to JetBrains and to open-source maintainers, and I build and maintain the free plugins in my catalog. Sponsorship pays for that time and keeps my work independent.

Sponsors

The first sponsor spots are open. Your name or logo could be the first one here.

What your sponsorship pays for

  • Fixes sent upstream. I write root-cause reports, patches and pull requests for the IntelliJ Platform and for open-source projects. The record is on the evidence pages.
  • Free tools that keep working. I keep the free plugins and extensions in my catalog compatible as the IDEs change.
  • New tools from documented gaps. Each plugin starts from one gap that reviews or tickets already describe.
  • Checks you can read. The tests and plugin-verifier runs behind each release, and the method I use to decide what to investigate.

What sponsorship never buys

  • A different conclusion. A sponsor cannot make me change, delay or remove an investigation, a report or a rating. If a sponsor’s own product is the subject, I still publish the report.
  • A place inside the tools. I never put sponsor logos or messages in my plugins or extensions.
  • Your data. I use the contact details you send only to arrange the sponsorship.
  • A services contract. A sponsorship has no fixed scope, deliverable or date. For that, see Services.

How to become a sponsor

  1. Pick a level. If you are unsure, start with Supporter or Backer and change later.
  2. Join. Monthly support goes through Patreon; a one-off contribution through Buy Me a Coffee. If your company needs an invoice or other paperwork, write to me first.
  3. Send your name or logo. For Silver and above, an SVG logo and the link you want. I add you to the page within a week.

Questions

Is a sponsorship tax-deductible?

No. I am not a registered charity, so a sponsorship is a personal or business payment, not a donation. Ask your accountant how to book it.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. Cancel from the platform you joined on. I take your name or logo off the site at the end of the period you already paid.

Can I sponsor anonymously?

Yes. Tell me, and you are counted but not listed.

Can I sponsor one plugin?

Tell me which one when you write. It guides where your priority requests go. It does not change what the plugin does or how it is reviewed.

How will I know what my sponsorship brought?

Gold and Platinum sponsors get a short note each quarter with the visits to the pages where the logo appears and the clicks on the logo. I count them with GoatCounter, which stores no personal data.

Are the links on this page followed by search engines?

Sponsor links are marked rel="sponsored", as search engines ask for paid placements.
