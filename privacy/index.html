Privacy Policy

What this site collects, what it doesn’t, and why the answer is short.

Effective date: September 24, 2026

The short version

Gap Hunter Labs runs no accounts, no payment processing, and no cookies. The only data collected about visitors is anonymous, aggregate traffic counts through a cookie-free analytics service. The “contact form” on this site doesn’t submit anywhere — it opens a pre-filled GitHub issue for you to send yourself. Everything below explains exactly what that means in practice.

Analytics

This site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count visits. GoatCounter does not use cookies or any persistent identifier, and does not track individual visitors across sessions or sites. It records aggregate figures — page views, referring site, browser/device class, and country-level location derived from IP address — without storing the IP address itself. You cannot be individually identified from this data, and neither can we identify you.

No other analytics, advertising, or tracking service runs on this site. There is no retargeting, no ad network, and no cross-site tracking pixel of any kind.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set cookies and does not use browser local storage or session storage for tracking or identification. Nothing is stored on your device by visiting this site.

The contact form

The forms on the Contact page (“Submit a gap,” “Report a plugin,” “Partnership”) have no backend. Submitting one does not send data to Gap Hunter Labs directly — it opens a pre-filled GitHub “new issue” page in a new tab, with your input placed into the issue title and body. Gap Hunter Labs only receives what you typed if you then choose to actually submit that issue on GitHub, at which point it becomes a public GitHub issue on a public repository, subject to GitHub’s own privacy policy and terms, and attributed to your GitHub account if you’re signed in.

If you’d rather not have your submission be public, email gaphunterlabs@gmail.com directly instead.

Third-party links

This site links out to JetBrains Marketplace, the VS Code Marketplace, and GitHub repositories for plugin installs, downloads, and source code. Once you leave gaphunterlabs.github.io, that platform’s own privacy policy applies — Gap Hunter Labs has no visibility into and no control over what those platforms collect.

What we never collect or publish

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no passwords.
  • No payment or billing information — plugin purchases happen entirely on JetBrains’ or Microsoft’s own marketplaces, never on this site.
  • No sale, rental, or sharing of any data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. There is essentially nothing collected that could be sold in the first place.

Your rights

Gap Hunter Labs is operated from Colombia by an individual, not a registered company. Colombian data protection law (Ley 1581 de 2012, “Habeas Data”) gives you the right to know, update, rectify, and request deletion of personal data held about you. In practice this site holds no personal data tied to an identifiable visitor to act on — the analytics above are aggregate and anonymous, and no other personal data is collected unless you choose to email us or submit a GitHub issue yourself. If you believe that’s not the case for something specific, contact us below and we’ll address it directly.

Children’s privacy

This site is a developer-tooling catalog and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age — see above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy: gaphunterlabs@gmail.com
